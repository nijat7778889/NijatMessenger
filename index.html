Installation

Install in five simple steps

The .ipa downloads straight to your iPhone. You just sign it once with iLoader.

  1. 1

    Download the IPA

    Open this page in Safari on your iPhone and tap Download .ipa. Safari saves it to the Files app.

  2. 2

    Open in Files

    In the Files app, go to Downloads and find NijatMessenger.ipa.

  3. 3

    Open iLoader

    Install iLoader if you haven't yet. Then share the file to iLoader, or open iLoader → My Apps → +.

  4. 4

    Install the app

    Select NijatMessenger.ipa in iLoader. It signs the app with your Apple ID and installs it to your Home Screen.

  5. 5

    Trust the developer

    Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, select the profile and tap Trust. Done — open the app.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The file opens a preview instead of downloading

Long-press the Download .ipa button and choose Download Linked File. Safari will then save it into the Files app.

It downloaded, but there's no Home Screen icon

That's expected. A raw .ipa doesn't install itself — open it in iLoader (step 4) so it gets signed and installed.

Do I need to sign in to iLoader?

Yes. iLoader signs apps with your personal Apple ID the first time you install or refresh them.

It says "Untrusted Developer"

Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, select the profile and tap Trust.

The app stopped opening after a week

Free Apple IDs limit signatures to 7 days. Open iLoader and tap Refresh to re-sign the app.
